🏃 RIDE WINNERS — Let Working Trades Reach Target
Breakeven Lock
Move stop-loss to entry once a trade earns its own risk back in profit
Relax Confidence-Exit After Breakeven Lock
Once locked, only a genuine signal reversal can exit — not routine confidence wobble
Trailing Stop
Keep trailing the stop behind price once a trade is running, beyond breakeven
These four settings work together to let a trade that's genuinely working keep running toward its take-profit target, instead of getting cut short by the same strict confidence checks that (correctly) protect a fresh, unproven trade. 1R = the trade's own initial risk distance (entry to original stop) — a trigger of 1.0R means "once up by as much as it originally risked." Once Breakeven Lock fires, the trade is structurally incapable of becoming a loss, so Relax Confidence-Exit stops treating routine indicator wobble as a reason to close it — only take-profit, the (now trailing) stop, or a genuine opposite-direction signal can end it from there.
🎯 SIGNAL QUALITY — Avoid Chop & Counter-Trend Entries
Chop Filter
Block new entries when the market is oscillating without net progress
Counter-Trend Discount
Scale down a signal that's fighting the prevailing EMA trend, proportional to how established it is
Chop Filter uses the Efficiency Ratio (net price movement ÷ total path length travelled, over the lookback window) — close to 1 means price moved efficiently in one direction (a real trend); close to 0 means lots of back-and-forth for little net progress. Below the threshold, new entries are capped below the confidence floor regardless of how strong other signals look — it never touches trades already open, which are protected separately by Ride Winners above.

Counter-Trend Discount reduces a signal's score when it's betting against an established EMA trend (e.g. a SELL signal while EMA21 is well above EMA50), scaled by how wide that separation actually is — a barely-separated, uncertain trend is discounted very little; a wide, established one gets close to the full discount. This only affects NEW entries, same as the chop filter.
Take profit is always recalculated as at least 4× the actual stop-loss distance, so the floor above is a minimum, not a fixed target — wider stops automatically get wider targets.

Max Execution Slippage guards against acting on a stale price: if the live market has moved more than this % since a signal was detected (RSI computation + AI confirmation both take real time), the bot skips that entry rather than execute at a price the signal was never actually based on. It re-checks next scan.

Signal Exit Confirmations requires the setup to fail this many consecutive scans in a row (not just one) before closing a trade early on lost confidence/direction — a single noisy scan won't cut a trade, but 2 in a row (default) means it's a real change. Stop-loss and take-profit are never affected by this — those remain instant, checked every cycle regardless of confirmations.

Hold Requires Entry Confidence raises the exit-confirmation bar per trade: if a trade opened at 90% confidence, it now needs to stay at or above 90% (not just the minimum floor above) to count as still valid. Dropping below its own entry confidence — even while still above the floor — starts the exit-confirmation clock as an early warning that conviction is weakening, before a full signal flip. Turn this off to fall back to the flat minimum-floor check only.
